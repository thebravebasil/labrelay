Turn research busywork into someone's first real project.

LabRelay connects professors and doctors who need help with manageable research tasks — literature reviews, data entry, survey outreach — with students who want real experience and a way in.

The exchange

One task board, two kinds of people who need each other.

For Researchers

1

Sign up, then post a task

Describe what needs doing and how long it should take. Takes under five minutes.

2

Review interested students

Read a short note from each student who applies, then pick who you'd like to work with.

3

Get the work back

The student delivers the finished task directly to you — no fee, no obligation beyond that.

For Students

1

Browse open tasks

Filter by time commitment and skills needed. No experience required to get started.

2

Sign up and apply with a note

Say why the task interests you. Researchers see this before they choose.

3

Do the work, build the relationship

Finish the task, and you've got real experience and a contact in the field.
