# LabRelay Roadmap
## 8th Grade → High School → College

_Last updated: August 2026_

LabRelay is a research-crowdsourcing platform designed to connect students who want research experience with legitimate researchers who need help with small, well-defined research tasks.

The long-term goal is to make a safe "first research experience" pathway for students while giving researchers a practical way to find beginner-friendly assistance.

---

## 1. Mission

**LabRelay:** Make legitimate research experience more accessible to students by matching them with researchers for safe, clearly scoped tasks.

### Core principle

LabRelay should optimize for:

- Safety
- Trust
- Beginner accessibility
- Research usefulness
- Privacy
- Skill development

# LabRelay Product Priorities

These are the highest-priority product areas.

## Priority 1 — Safety

Before growth:

- [ ] Establish age/guardian rules.
- [ ] Define what kinds of research tasks are allowed.
- [ ] Block sensitive patient information.
- [ ] Add reporting mechanisms.
- [ ] Add moderation workflows.
- [ ] Avoid uncontrolled private communication between minors and adults.
- [ ] Review privacy/security rules before real-world deployment.

## Priority 2 — Verification

Researchers should have a visible trust state:

- Unverified
- Identity/affiliation submitted
- Affiliation reviewed
- Verified researcher

Verification should never imply that LabRelay guarantees the quality of a study.

## Priority 3 — Better task structure

Every opportunity should have:

- Research topic
- Task type
- Description
- Estimated hours
- Duration
- Required skills
- Difficulty
- Remote/in-person status
- Data sensitivity
- Supervision requirements
- Eligibility
- Researcher verification status

## Priority 4 — Matching

Build a transparent matching score rather than a mysterious AI score.

Example:

**Match: 86%**

- Skills: 35/40
- Time: 20/20
- Difficulty: 15/20
- Topic interest: 16/20

Explain the score to users.

## Priority 5 — Evidence of impact

Track metrics such as:

- Opportunities posted
- Applications submitted
- Matches made
- Projects completed
- Students gaining first research experience
- Researcher satisfaction

Do not collect sensitive data just to produce metrics.

# Technical Development Order

1. Stabilize current application
2. Security and Firebase rules review
3. User roles
4. Researcher verification state
5. Task safety metadata
6. Reporting/moderation
7. Structured profiles
8. Matching engine
9. Analytics
10. AI-assisted features

**Important:** AI should come after the core workflow is safe and reliable. A sophisticated AI feature cannot compensate for weak access control or unsafe data handling.

# Definition of Success

Do not judge LabRelay mainly by the number of features.

A successful early LabRelay prototype is one where:

**A student can safely understand an opportunity, a researcher can clearly describe a small task, and both sides know what happens next.**

That is the core product.
