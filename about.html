Why this exists

Research needs hands. Students need a way in.

Small research tasks — screening papers, entering data, recruiting participants — take up real time that could go toward the actual science. Meanwhile, students who want to work in medicine or research rarely get a foot in the door before college. LabRelay is a small, direct way to close that gap: real tasks, real researchers, real experience.

"I'm building this because I want to work in healthcare technology someday, and I figured the best way to learn what researchers actually need was to build something that makes their work a little easier — and put myself in the room to ask."

Mohammad Basil

Founder, rising 8th grader · Clarksburg, MD
