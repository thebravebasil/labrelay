Why this exists

Research needs hands. Students need a way in.

Small research tasks — screening papers, entering data, recruiting participants — take up real time that could go toward the actual science. Meanwhile, students who want to work in research face a barrier: no connections, no experience, no obvious way in.

LabRelay exists to fix that gap. It's a direct connection between researchers who need help and students who want real experience. No fees. No middleman. Just work that matters.

For researchers: Post a task, review applications, pick someone, and get the work done.

For students: Browse real research, apply with a note about why you're interested, and build a network in the field.

"I'm building this because I want to work in healthcare technology someday, and I figured the best way to learn what researchers actually need was to build something that makes their work easier. Plus, I remember being a middle schooler who wanted to do research but had no idea how to start. This is for everyone in that position."

Mohammad Basil

Founder, rising 8th grader

How it works

The three-step exchange

For Researchers

1. Post a task — Describe what needs doing: screening papers, data entry, survey outreach, etc. Estimate how long it should take. Done in 5 minutes.

2. Review applications — Students read your task and apply with a short note about why it interests them. You see each applicant's name and contact info.

3. Get the work back — Pick who you want to work with, send them the details, and they complete the task. No fee, no obligation.

For Students

1. Browse and explore — See open tasks from real researchers. Filter by time commitment and type of work. No experience required.

2. Apply with a story — Click "I'm interested" and write a short note about why. It's not a resume — tell the researcher what you actually care about.

3. Do real work — If they pick you, you'll get their email and the details. Complete the task, and you've got real experience, a portfolio piece, and a contact in the field.

Privacy & Trust

What happens to your info

Your name and email are only shown to the person you're working with. A researcher sees an applicant's info only once they apply. A student sees a researcher's email only once they're accepted.

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. We don't use it for marketing. Your information stays between you and the person on the other side of the task.

We use Firebase for hosting and auth. It's SOC 2 compliant and follows industry-standard security practices. All data is encrypted in transit.

The basics

  • No account fees — LabRelay is free forever.
  • No task fees — Researchers don't pay to post. Students don't pay to apply.
  • No money changes hands on LabRelay — Compensation is between researcher and student, paid directly.
  • You own your data — Delete your account anytime and all your info goes with it.
  • Be honest — We ask researchers to be truthful about who they are. Students will Google you.
FAQ

Questions we hear

Is this really free?

Yes. No fees for researchers. No fees for students. No ads. We built this as a side project to solve a real problem.

Do I need a PhD or credentials to post?

No required credentials. But be honest about who you are — students will do their homework before applying. If you're a grad student running a study, that's fine. If you're a professor, even better. Just be real about it.

What if I don't finish the task?

Communication is key. Agree on scope, timeline, and expectations upfront. If something changes, talk to the other person. We're exploring ways to help verify completion and resolve disputes, but right now it's based on trust and direct communication.

How much time should I budget for a task?

Tasks range from 1–2 hours (quick lit review) to 10+ hours (large data entry project). You pick the time commitment when you post. Be realistic — students trust that estimate.

Do you take a cut if I pay a student?

No. LabRelay is free to use. If you decide to pay someone (which we encourage), that's entirely between you and them. We don't take any money.

How is this different from Upwork or Fiverr?

Those platforms charge 10–20% fees and focus on freelancing. LabRelay is built specifically for research tasks, completely free, and focused on direct connection between researchers and students. You don't need a portfolio or fancy profile — just a genuine interest in the work.

Can I message the other person before accepting?

Not yet. Students can write a note when they apply, and you can see that note. Direct messaging is on the roadmap.

What if someone is scamming?

Trust is built on reputation. We're adding reviews and ratings so users can see who's trustworthy. For now, Google the researcher before you apply. Check their university email, their publications, their lab website. Be skeptical but open-minded.
